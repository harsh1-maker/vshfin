OUR METHOD

Five steps from dirty tank to clean handover

Most household tanks of 500–2,000 litres can be completed in about 45–60 minutes, depending on access and condition.

1

Drain

Remove remaining water so the tank floor and walls are accessible.

2

Remove sludge

Lift out settled mud, silt and slime instead of redistributing it.

3

Scrub & pressure wash

Clean walls, floor, corners, lid and inlet thoroughly.

4

Disinfect

Apply an appropriate disinfecting treatment and rinse as required.

5

Photo proof

Share before-and-after photos and complete the handover.

FAQ

Common tank-cleaning questions

How often should a water tank be cleaned?

Many households schedule cleaning around every 6–12 months, with more frequent cleaning where tanks collect visible sediment or usage conditions demand it.

Do you clean underground tanks?

Yes. Underground sumps and larger reservoirs can be booked using the underground tank service and confirmed according to access and size.

Do I need to empty the tank before the team arrives?

No. The cleaning process includes draining remaining water where access and site conditions permit.

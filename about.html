OUR STORY

A Better Approach To Tank Cleaning.

Water storage tanks are often out of sight and can easily be overlooked. VSH exists to make professional tank cleaning easier to book and easier to understand.

Instead of treating tank cleaning as a quick wash, we focus on a structured process that includes draining, sludge removal, scrubbing, washing, hygiene treatment and final inspection.
